# Purchase conversion rate

For each acquisition channel, divide total completed purchases by total
sessions. Return a fraction between zero and one, rather than a percentage.
Purchases reconcile with completed retail orders for the same date and channel.
The sample gives paid social more sessions and spend but lower conversion
than direct traffic. The certified function returns one row per channel.
